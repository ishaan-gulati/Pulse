Our Story

Built by a teen,
for teens.

Pulse started because I wanted to learn investing — but everything out there was either too boring, too complicated, or had no social element. So I built it myself.

The Problem

Gen Z has nowhere to learn investing.

Robinhood and Fidelity aren't built for teens. No social layer, no gamification, no community — and you need real money just to start.

Paper trading apps exist but they're dead inside. Gen Z learns by doing, competing, sharing. There was no investing app built that way. Until now.

The Solution

Paper trading that's
actually fun.

Pulse gives you $10,000 in virtual money to trade real stocks with live market data. Post picks, follow other traders, climb the leaderboard, earn XP for every move.

All the experience of real investing. None of the risk.

Ishaan Gulati

Founder & Developer

High school freshman from Virginia. Built Pulse entirely solo. Previously won Best Mobile App at HackTJ with Fern AI — a tool that scans hospital bills for errors and flags duplicate charges. Passionate about fintech, entrepreneurship, and building things people actually use.

The mission

To make financial literacy accessible, competitive, and fun for every teenager — regardless of their background or how much money they have.
