Privacy Policy

Last Updated May 25, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Pulse ("the App"), a gamified social paper trading app developed and operated by Ishaan Gulati ("I", "me", or "my"). This Privacy Policy explains how I collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use Pulse.

By using Pulse, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

⚠️ Paper Trading Only. Pulse uses paper trading only. No real money is ever invested or at risk. The App is intended for educational and entertainment purposes.

Information I Collect

  • Account Information: Your name and email address when you create an account.
  • Profile Picture: If you choose to upload a profile picture, that image is stored securely via Firebase Storage. Uploading a profile picture is optional — you may use Pulse without one.
  • Usage Data: Features used, trades placed, pages visited, session duration, and in-app actions — collected automatically via Firebase.
  • Device Information: Device type, OS version, and unique device identifiers collected by Firebase.
  • User-Generated Content: Trading activity, portfolio data, posts, and leaderboard entries you create.

I do not collect financial account numbers, payment information, Social Security numbers, real-time location, or health data.

How I Use Your Information

  • Create and manage your account
  • Provide, operate, and improve the App
  • Display leaderboards, XP, and social feed content
  • Analyze usage trends to improve user experience
  • Send important service-related communications
  • Monitor and maintain the security and integrity of the App
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

Third-Party Services

I use Google Firebase for authentication, database storage, and analytics. Firebase may collect device identifiers and usage data per Google's Privacy Policy.

I do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. I do not use advertising networks or ad SDKs. Data may be shared only to comply with legal obligations, protect user safety, or with your explicit consent.

I do not share data from users under 18 with any third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Retention and Deletion

I retain your information while your account is active. You may request deletion at any time from within the App via Settings → Account → Delete Account, or by contacting me directly. Deletion requests are processed within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

Pulse is intended for users aged 13 and older. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, please contact me immediately and I will delete it promptly.

App Tracking Transparency

Pulse does not track users across third-party apps or websites for advertising purposes. Firebase Analytics is used solely to improve the App experience and does not collect identifiable information from minors.

Data Security

I implement appropriate technical measures to protect your data including secure Firebase authentication, encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS), and restricted access controls. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Your Rights

  • Access the personal data I hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Delete your account and personal data
  • Withdraw consent for data collection
  • Opt out of non-essential communications

Changes to This Policy

I may update this policy from time to time. I will revise the "Last Updated" date and notify you via the App or email where appropriate.
