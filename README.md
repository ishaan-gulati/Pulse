## What is Pulse?

**Pulse** is a free paper trading app designed for teenagers and young investors who want to learn the stock market without risking real money. Using **live market data**, Pulse lets you simulate buying and selling real stocks — then compete with friends on leaderboards, earn XP, and build a track record before you ever touch a real brokerage.

Think: **Duolingo meets Robinhood**, built for Gen Z.

---

## Why Pulse?

Most investing apps are built for adults. Teens are left out — or worse, thrown into real trading with no experience.

Pulse fixes that by making **financial literacy fun**:

- 🏆 **Compete** — Climb weekly leaderboards against friends and the global community
- 📊 **Learn** — Trade real tickers (AAPL, TSLA, NVDA) with real price data, zero risk
- 🤝 **Connect** — Follow friends, see their portfolios, and share your picks on the social feed
- 🎮 **Level up** — Earn XP and badges as you grow your investing skills
- 📱 **Track** — Monitor your simulated portfolio performance over time
